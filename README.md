One line, one cat, zero emails answered: my inbox is being watched by a cat who has already sat on your email, knocked it off the desk, and gone back to sleep.

> "I'm away until Monday — my inbox is being watched by my cat, who has already sat on your email, knocked it off the desk, and gone back to sleep."

## How it works

`main.py` holds the entry line as a constant, checks it against the kit's gate (UTF-8 text, 1–160 characters, not only whitespace, a single line with no control characters or padding), prints it, and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` when run with `python3 main.py`. Run `python3 main.py --self-test` to verify the printed line is byte-identical to the entry.

## Entry

- Competition: [Funniest One-Line Out-of-Office Reply](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hamsyh-funniest-one-line-out-of-office-reply-5490c1c8)
- Entry: 144 characters, one line.
- Why it works: it reads like a real away message — a return date, a named stand-in — and the stand-in is a cat doing exactly what cats do to anything left on a desk. The laugh lands on the first read, with no setup to explain.
